Privacy Policy for KINA MOVIE

Last updated: July 2026

At KINA MOVIE, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by KINA MOVIE and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data that you provide directly, such as when you type comments on our video selection. We also use standard web analytics to see how many people visit our site.

2. Log Files

KINA MOVIE follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and number of clicks.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to KINA MOVIE and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on KINA MOVIE, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites.

5. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. KINA MOVIE does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

6. Consent
